Our Story

A café built around cats

Cat Coffe started with a simple idea: coffee tastes better when a cat is nearby. We built a calm, cozy room where people can slow down, enjoy a good drink, and spend time with our resident cats.

We keep the space gentle — soft voices, no flash photography, and plenty of sunny spots for naps. Whether you come alone, with friends, or with family, we hope you leave a little lighter than when you arrived.
